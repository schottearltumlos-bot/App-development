# 🎬 My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## 📖 Overview
> *"Friends don't lie."* — Eleven

Stranger Things is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, the story follows a group of friends and their mysterious encounters with supernatural forces and a parallel dimension known as the **Upside Down**.

---

## 🌟 Why I Love It

- **✨ Great 80s Nostalgia** — The music, fashion, and references make it feel timeless
- **👥 Amazing Characters** — Each character brings something unique to the group
- **🕵️ Mystery & Suspense** — Every season keeps you guessing
- **❤️ Heartwarming Friendship** — The bond between the characters is truly inspiring
